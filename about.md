I'm Ben, an Associate Lecturer at UCL focusing on materials development for our [CLIE Pre-sessional Course](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/languages-international-education/preparation-courses/pre-sessional-english-courses){target="_blank"}.

I am also a part-time student, which is why I started this page. I am studying at the University of St. Andrews, and through my Doctoral Studies (to become a [Doctor of Professional Practice](https://www.st-andrews.ac.uk/subjects/tesol/tesol-dprof-september-and-january/){target="_blank"}), I have set myself the ambitious goal of completing high quality research studies that are practice focused. That is, I hope to use research projects and methods to improve the quality of the pre-sessional course in terms of teaching and learning, as well as the student and teacher experience. 

My main interest is in understanding how students tackle the demands of a pre-sessional research project that involves writing from sources.
